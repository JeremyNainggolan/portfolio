Hi, I'm Jeremy Mart V. Nainggolan — an Information Systems student at Del Institute of Technology with a deep passion for software engineering.

Over the past few years, I have developed strong expertise across the full software development lifecycle, including web development, back-end engineering, and cross-platform mobile application development.

Indonesia AirAsia using Flutter and Laravel, as well as independently developing warehouse management applications during my time at PT. Younexa Inti Materials.
